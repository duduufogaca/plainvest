How the investment simulator works

The simulator combines three forces that build wealth: your starting amount, your regular contributions, and compound growth over time. Each year your money earns a return, and the next year that return earns its own return. Over decades, this snowball effect does most of the heavy lifting — not the size of any single deposit.

💡 The biggest driver of long-term results isn't picking winners — it's time in the market and consistency. Starting earlier with a smaller amount often beats starting later with more.

What the numbers mean

  • Starting amount — what you invest today as a lump sum.
  • Monthly contribution — what you add each month, automatically.
  • Years invested — your time horizon; longer horizons compound far more.
  • Average annual return — a long-term assumption (a diversified portfolio has historically averaged ~7–8%).

Why visualizing your future matters

Most people never invest because the future feels abstract. Seeing a concrete number — what $300 a month could become in 20 years — turns "someday" into a decision you can make today. That's the whole idea behind Plainvest: future clarity, smarter decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What is an investment simulator?

It estimates how a starting amount plus regular contributions could grow over time at an assumed annual return — helping you visualize compound growth before investing real money.

What return rate should I use?

A diversified long-term portfolio has historically returned roughly 7–8% per year before inflation. These are educational assumptions, not guarantees — real returns vary year to year.

Is the Plainvest simulator free?

Yes — completely free, no account required. You only sign up if you want the full member hub, guides and portfolio tools.

Does this predict my exact returns?

No. It's an educational projection at a fixed assumed rate. Markets rise and fall — it shows the power of consistency and time, not a promised outcome.
