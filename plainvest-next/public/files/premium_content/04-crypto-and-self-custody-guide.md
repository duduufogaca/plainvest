# Premium Guide: Crypto and Self-Custody

## Important warning

Crypto has unique risks. Mistakes can be permanent. This guide is educational and should be read before buying or moving crypto.

## Exchange vs wallet

An exchange account is like a platform login. It lets you buy and sell crypto.

A wallet is software or hardware that controls crypto keys.

Self-custody means you are responsible for protecting your own keys.

## Key terms

- private key: proves control of crypto
- seed phrase: backup words that can restore a wallet
- hot wallet: connected to the internet
- cold wallet: kept offline, often hardware-based
- transaction fee: cost to move crypto on-chain

## Self-custody rules

Never:

- type seed phrases into random websites
- store seed phrases in screenshots
- share private keys
- trust DMs offering help
- rush a withdrawal

Always:

- test with small amounts first
- check the address carefully
- use official wallet sources
- keep backups offline
- understand the recovery process

## Common beginner mistakes

- buying before understanding storage
- sending coins to the wrong network
- believing guaranteed-return offers
- using weak passwords
- ignoring tax records

## Member action

Before buying crypto, answer:

- Why am I buying this?
- How much could I lose without panic?
- Will I keep it on exchange or wallet?
- Do I understand recovery?
