Why Plainvest was created

Plainvest started from a simple frustration: most people are told they should invest, but they are not shown the first steps in a way that feels human. The internet has endless videos, opinions, charts, products, and predictions. For a beginner, more content often creates more hesitation.

The idea behind Plainvest is to make the first learning experience visual and simple. You change the numbers, compare paths, see inflation, and then learn the words after the numbers already make sense.

Premium exists for the next step. It is for members who want more than a calculator: they want a reading path, practical checklists, crypto safety, exchange guidance, book recommendations, and a place to organize questions before acting.

The goal is confidence, not pressure. Plainvest does not tell you what to buy. It helps you understand what you are looking at, what can go wrong, and what to check before money is involved.

What members are really paying for

The value is not only access to information. Most investing information already exists somewhere online. The value is structure: what to learn first, what to ignore for now, which risks matter, how to avoid common mistakes, and how to turn confusing terms into practical decisions.

How to use this membership well

One topic at a time

Do not jump from ETFs to Bitcoin to macro in one sitting. Pick one module, understand it, then compare it with the simulator.

Write questions down

Every time something feels unclear, save it. Good questions make the support call much more useful.

Separate learning from buying

Learning should feel calm. Buying decisions should come only after risks, fees, taxes, and personal goals are understood.

Use the books as depth

The book list is there when you want stronger foundations in behaviour, market history, Bitcoin, macro, and business thinking.

Platform access direction
