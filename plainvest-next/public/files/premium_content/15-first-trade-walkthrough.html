First Trade

From ready to click to actually understanding the click.

This guide does not tell you what to buy. It explains the practical steps beginners usually freeze on when placing a first ETF or share order.

The calm walkthrough

  1. Confirm your learning decision. Know why you picked the investment type, not just the ticker.
  2. Fund the account. Make a small test deposit first and confirm it appears correctly.
  3. Search the ticker. Check name, exchange, currency, and whether it matches the product you intended.
  4. Choose order type. Market orders prioritise execution. Limit orders set the maximum price you are willing to pay.
  5. Review fees. Check brokerage, FX fees, and the total estimated cost before confirming.
  6. Download confirmation. Save trade confirmation and statements for tax records.

Market vs limit order

Market order

Buys quickly at the best available price. Simple, but final price can move, especially in volatile assets.

Limit order

You set the highest price you accept. It may not fill, but it gives more control.

First-trade rule

For a beginner, the first trade should be about learning the process, not proving confidence. Start small enough that a mistake becomes a lesson, not a crisis.
