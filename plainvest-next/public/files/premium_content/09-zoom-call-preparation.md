# Premium Guide: 60-Minute Zoom Call Preparation

## Purpose

Premium includes one 60-minute Zoom call during the access year. The call is for education, questions, platform understanding, and confidence-building.

It is not personal financial advice.

## Good topics for the call

- understanding investment types
- choosing what to learn first
- exchange or platform questions
- DCA planning
- book path questions
- Bitcoin and self-custody doubts
- chart-reading confusion
- risk and volatility questions

## Before the call

Prepare:

- your main goal
- your country
- your experience level
- investments you are curious about
- questions from the simulator
- questions from the guides
- any platform confusion

## Suggested call structure

First 10 minutes:

- goals and current confusion

Next 20 minutes:

- explain the topic or platform question

Next 20 minutes:

- walk through examples, risks, and next learning steps

Final 10 minutes:

- summarize action list and follow-up resources

## Questions to bring

- What should I understand before using this platform?
- What risks am I missing?
- What is the simplest next learning step?
- Which guide should I read next?
- What should I avoid rushing?
