# Premium Guide: Exchange and Platform Setup

## Purpose

This checklist helps members prepare before opening or using an investing platform, brokerage, or crypto exchange.

## Before choosing a platform

Check:

- country availability
- regulation or licensing
- fees
- available investment products
- deposit and withdrawal options
- customer support
- security tools
- tax reports or statements

## Account setup checklist

Prepare:

- legal name
- email address
- phone number
- ID document
- proof of address if required
- bank account details
- strong password
- two-factor authentication

## Security checklist

Use:

- unique password
- password manager
- two-factor authentication
- withdrawal whitelist if available
- anti-phishing code if available
- device security

Avoid:

- reusing passwords
- sharing login details
- clicking sponsored links without checking the URL
- rushing KYC or deposit steps

## Understanding orders

Common terms:

- market order: buys immediately at market price
- limit order: buys only at your chosen price or better
- spread: difference between buy and sell price
- fee: cost charged by the platform
- recurring buy: automatic repeat purchase

## Member action

Before depositing money, write:

- why I chose this platform
- what the fees are
- how I withdraw money
- how my account is protected
