Glossary

60 investing words in plain English.

Use this when a guide, broker, chart, or book uses a word that makes the whole thing feel harder than it needs to be.

Core terms

ETF

A fund traded on an exchange like a share.

Index fund

A fund designed to track a market index.

Expense ratio

The yearly fund fee as a percentage.

Brokerage

The fee charged to place a trade.

Dividend

Cash paid by a company or fund to investors.

Franking credit

An Australian tax credit attached to some dividends.

DCA

Investing a fixed amount regularly over time.

Liquidity

How easily something can be bought or sold.

Volatility

How sharply prices move up and down.

Drawdown

The fall from a previous high to a lower value.

Rebalancing

Adjusting a portfolio back to target weights.

CGT

Tax rules that can apply when selling an asset for a gain.
