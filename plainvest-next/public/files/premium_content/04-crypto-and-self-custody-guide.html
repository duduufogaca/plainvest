Self-custody basicsFundamentos da autocustódia

Self-custody means you control the keys that control the asset. That freedom comes with responsibility. If a seed phrase is lost, stolen, photographed, stored in cloud notes, or typed into a fake site, recovery may be impossible.

  • Never type your seed phrase into a website or app unless restoring a wallet you intentionally installed from the official source.Nunca digite sua seed phrase em um site ou aplicativo, a menos que esteja restaurando uma carteira instalada intencionalmente a partir da fonte oficial.
  • Keep backups offline, private, and protected from water, fire, and theft.Mantenha backups offline, privados e protegidos contra água, fogo e roubo.
  • Test small transactions before sending meaningful amounts.Teste com transações pequenas antes de enviar valores significativos.
  • Check network names carefully: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, and other networks are different rails.Verifique os nomes das redes com cuidado: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Polygon e outras redes são trilhos diferentes.
  • Assume DMs, giveaways, urgent support messages, and “double your money” offers are dangerous.

Beginner mistakes to avoidErros comuns a evitar

Buying because price is moving

Fast green candles can create fear of missing out. That is not a plan.

Using leverage

Leverage can liquidate beginners quickly. It is not needed for learning.

Ignoring withdrawal tests

A small test withdrawal can reveal address, network, or platform issues before a larger mistake.

Confusing project marketing with value

Token supply, insiders, real usage, security, liquidity, and incentives matter more than hype.

Recovery phrase rulesRegras da frase de recuperação

  • Write it offline. Do not screenshot it.
  • Do not store it in email, cloud notes, WhatsApp, photo gallery, or password manager unless you fully understand the risk model.
  • Never share it with support. Real support does not need it.
  • Consider metal backup for meaningful long-term holdings.
  • Test recovery with tiny amounts before trusting a setup with larger value.

Hardware wallets: Ledger vs TrezorCarteiras de hardware: Ledger vs Trezor

A hardware wallet is a small physical device that stores your private keys offline. It signs transactions without exposing the key to an internet-connected device. For any meaningful long-term crypto holding, hardware wallets are considered best practice. The two most established brands are Ledger and Trezor.

FeatureLedger (Nano S Plus / X / Flex)Trezor (Model One / Safe 3 / Safe 5)

Key point: both are reputable. Neither brand guarantees safety if you share your seed phrase, buy from unofficial sources, or skip the setup verification process. Always buy directly from the manufacturer's official website — never from resellers on Amazon or other marketplaces where devices could be pre-configured.

Hardware wallet setup checklist

  • Buy only from the manufacturer's official website.
  • Verify the packaging seal is intact and device shows no sign of prior setup.
  • Set up the device on the official app (Ledger Live or Trezor Suite) downloaded from the official site.
  • Write the seed phrase offline — pen and paper, never photographed or typed anywhere.
  • Verify the seed phrase by restoring to a test device or using the device's own verification function before storing value.
  • Store the seed phrase in a separate physical location from the device.
  • Test a tiny transaction before transferring meaningful amounts.

Crypto inheritance: the question most beginners skipHerança cripto: a pergunta que a maioria dos iniciantes ignora

If you self-custody crypto and something happens to you, the asset is gone unless you have made deliberate plans. Unlike a bank account, there is no recovery process, no beneficiary form, and no customer support. This is not a morbid topic — it is a responsibility that comes with self-custody.

The problem

A seed phrase stored only in your memory or in a single hidden location that only you know about is permanently inaccessible if you die or are incapacitated. Courts, lawyers, and family cannot recover it.

Basic plan

At minimum: write a document that tells a trusted person that crypto exists, what wallets or exchanges hold it, and where to find access instructions. Keep this document physically secure and updated.

Seed phrase storage options

Split the seed phrase into two parts stored in separate secure locations (requires both parts to restore). Or use a sealed envelope in a fireproof safe with a trusted family member. Metal backup plates (Cryptosteel, Bilodeau) resist fire and water.

Get professional help for large holdings

For meaningful value, consider a solicitor who understands digital assets, or a crypto inheritance specialist. Some hardware wallet manufacturers publish inheritance guides. This is not optional at significant size.

Popular hot wallets: what beginners actually useHot wallets populares: o que os iniciantes realmente usam

A hot wallet is software that stores your private keys on an internet-connected device (phone or computer). Hot wallets give you direct control of your crypto, but they depend on device security and your own caution. These are the most widely used options — but any wallet is only as safe as the person using it.

Hot wallet rule: never keep more than you would keep in a physical wallet in cash on your hot wallet. Hot wallets are for regular use and learning transactions. Meaningful long-term holdings belong in cold storage once you have practiced the setup.

Network compatibility: why this matters before you send

Every crypto asset lives on a specific network. Sending to the wrong network or using the wrong address format is one of the most common irreversible mistakes beginners make.

NetworkCommon assetsWhat to check before sending
Bitcoin (BTC)Bitcoin onlyBitcoin addresses start with 1, 3, or bc1. Confirm the receiving address format matches. Never send BTC to an Ethereum address.
Ethereum (ETH / ERC-20)ETH, USDC, USDT (ERC-20), thousands of tokensAll Ethereum addresses start with 0x. Confirm the token contract is the official one (check on Etherscan) before sending.
Solana (SOL / SPL tokens)SOL, USDC (Solana), BONK, many othersSolana addresses are base58 encoded (32-44 chars). Very fast and cheap, but completely irreversible. Use Solscan.io to verify.
BNB Chain (BEP-20)BNB, USDT (BEP-20), BUSDSame address format as Ethereum (0x) but different network. Sending BEP-20 to an ERC-20 address on the wrong network can result in loss.
Tron (TRC-20)USDT (TRC-20) widely used for low-fee transfersTron addresses start with T. USDT on Tron and USDT on Ethereum are different networks — confirm which one the exchange supports before withdrawing.
Before every first transfer: open the destination platform's help page, confirm which network they support for that asset, then match the sending network in your wallet. A test transfer with the minimum amount is always worth the extra few minutes.

Transaction safety workflowFluxo de segurança em transações

  1. Confirm the asset and network. USDT on Ethereum, Tron, Solana, and Polygon are not the same path.
  2. Copy the address from the destination wallet and check the first and last characters after pasting.
  3. Send a tiny test transaction first when using a new wallet, exchange, or network.
  4. Wait for confirmation before sending more. Do not rush because of fees or impatience.
  5. Record what you did: platform, network, date, transaction ID, and purpose.

Scam patterns to recognise immediatelyPadrões de golpes para reconhecer imediatamente

Crypto scams follow repeatable patterns. Recognising the pattern is faster than evaluating each situation individually.

PatternHow it looksWhat to do
Seed phrase requestSupport agent, browser pop-up, or verification form asks for your 12 or 24 recovery words.Stop completely. No legitimate service ever needs your seed phrase. It is always a theft attempt.
Fake wallet appA search result or app store listing for Ledger, MetaMask, or Trust Wallet that is not the official source.Only install from the official website. Check the developer name and number of reviews critically.
Approval phishingA DeFi site asks you to approve a transaction that gives it control over all tokens in your wallet.Revoke approvals at revoke.cash. Never approve unlimited spend limits on unknown contracts.
Fake giveaway"Send 0.1 BTC and receive 0.2 back." Celebrity or project impersonation. Always a scam.No legitimate giveaway requires sending first. Block and report.
Urgent support DMSomeone DMs you on Twitter, Discord, or Telegram claiming to be support after you post a question publicly.Real support does not DM unsolicited. Never follow links from DMs about your wallet or account.
Address poisoningA scammer sends a tiny transaction from an address visually similar to one you use, hoping you copy-paste it next time.Always check the full address character by character before sending. Never copy from transaction history.
Pig butcheringA stranger builds a relationship over weeks, then introduces a "highly profitable" trading platform requiring deposits.Any romantic or social contact that eventually leads to a trading platform recommendation is a fraud operation.

Wallet practice plan

Members should practice with very small amounts before treating self-custody as a storage solution. The goal is to build operational confidence before the value becomes emotionally meaningful.

  1. Create a wallet from an official source and write the recovery phrase offline.
  2. Receive a tiny amount from an exchange or another wallet.
  3. Send a tiny amount back out and confirm network fees and confirmation time.
  4. Restore the wallet on a spare device or safe test environment before relying on the backup.
  5. Only after practice, decide whether hot wallet, cold wallet, or exchange custody matches the risk level.
