# Premium Guide: Chart Reading and Research Tools

## Purpose

Charts help members observe price behavior, but they should not become gambling tools. Use charts for context, not certainty.

## Beginner chart terms

- trend: general direction
- support: area where buyers may appear
- resistance: area where sellers may appear
- volume: how much is being traded
- moving average: smoothed price over time
- drawdown: fall from a previous high

## What charts can help with

Charts can help members:

- understand volatility
- avoid chasing vertical moves
- see long-term cycles
- compare assets
- plan entries with more patience

## What charts cannot do

Charts cannot:

- guarantee future price
- remove risk
- replace research
- protect against bad position sizing

## Simple research process

1. Understand the asset.
2. Check the long-term trend.
3. Check volatility and drawdowns.
4. Check fees and taxes.
5. Decide whether the risk fits your plan.

## Content channels

When watching finance content, ask:

- Is this educational or selling urgency?
- Are risks mentioned?
- Is the person transparent about uncertainty?
- Does the content explain process or only predictions?

## Member action

Before acting on a chart, write one sentence:

“This chart is interesting because ______, but the main risk is ______.”
