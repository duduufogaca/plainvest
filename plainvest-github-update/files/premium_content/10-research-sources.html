Premium Sources

Research sources and study map.

References are kept separate from the learning pages so the guides stay clean. Use this page when you want to verify ideas, go deeper, or build future Plainvest modules.

Investor education and portfolio foundations

  • Investor.gov: diversification, dollar-cost averaging, funds, fees, risk, and investor basics.
  • ASIC Moneysmart: diversification, risk and return, investment scams, and beginner education.
  • Vanguard education: goals, balance, cost, and discipline as long-term investing principles.
  • Bogleheads investment policy statement: asset allocation, index investing, and simple portfolio thinking.
  • SEC investor resources: investor alerts, fraud warnings, funds, fees, and market education.
  • Books: The Little Book of Common Sense Investing, A Random Walk Down Wall Street, The Psychology of Money, The Intelligent Investor, Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits.

Bitcoin, crypto, custody, and cycles

  • Bitcoin white paper and Bitcoin.org education for peer-to-peer cash, proof-of-work, nodes, and wallet basics.
  • FTC crypto scam guidance for recovery phrases, fake support, phishing, and fraud patterns.
  • Glassnode Academy for realized price, exchange balances, hash rate, fees, and cycle context.
  • TradingView for chart reading, watchlists, alerts, and multi-asset market context.
  • Books: The Bitcoin Standard, Inventing Bitcoin, Broken Money, The Price of Tomorrow, Mastering Bitcoin for technical curiosity.
  • Cycle study: past Bitcoin and crypto market drawdowns, liquidity changes, narrative rotation, and custody failures.

Macro, inflation, risk, and decision-making

  • Federal Reserve Education for inflation, interest rates, money, and economic basics.
  • Reserve Bank of Australia education for inflation, monetary policy, and the Australian context.
  • Bank for International Settlements for deeper central bank and monetary system research.
  • Books: The Black Swan, Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order, The Price of Tomorrow, Fooled by Randomness.
  • Behaviour and uncertainty study: position sizing, risk of ruin, emotional decision-making, and why overconfidence hurts investors.

How Plainvest should use sources

Sources should support trust, but the member experience should stay simple. The guides should explain concepts in Plainvest language, while this source page keeps the research trail separate for members who want to verify or go deeper.
