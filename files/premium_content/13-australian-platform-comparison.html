Decision filters

Cost

Brokerage, FX spread, account fees, inactivity fees, and fund management fees.

Assets

Can you buy the ETFs, shares, or markets you actually want?

Ownership model

Understand CHESS sponsorship, custodian models, and what your statements look like.

Ease of use

The best platform for a beginner is often the one they can use safely and consistently.

Before opening an account

  • Read the current fee schedule on the official platform site.
  • Check minimum deposit and brokerage rules.
  • Confirm whether the platform supports your target ETF or investment.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Make one small test deposit before moving larger amounts.
