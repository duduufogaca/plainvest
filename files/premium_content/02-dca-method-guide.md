# Premium Guide: DCA Method

## What DCA means

DCA stands for dollar-cost averaging. It means investing a planned amount on a regular schedule instead of trying to guess the perfect day to buy.

Examples:

- AUD $100 every month
- BRL R$200 every month
- USD $50 every week

## Why beginners use it

DCA helps reduce the stress of timing the market.

Instead of asking, “Is today the perfect day?”, the member asks:

- Can I afford this contribution?
- Does this match my long-term goal?
- Can I keep doing this consistently?

## Simple DCA plan

1. Choose an amount you can repeat.
2. Choose a frequency.
3. Choose one broad investment path first.
4. Automate the contribution if possible.
5. Review every 3-6 months, not every day.

## Mistakes to avoid

- starting with an amount that is too high
- stopping because the market drops
- changing strategy every week
- investing money needed for bills or emergencies
- using DCA as an excuse to avoid learning risk

## Reflection questions

- What amount can I invest without pressure?
- What would make me stop?
- Do I understand what I am buying?
- Is my emergency fund separate?
