# Premium Guide: Bitcoin Research Section

## Why Bitcoin was created

Bitcoin was created as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system that does not depend on a central bank or single company.

It appeared after the 2008 financial crisis, when trust in banks, bailouts, and monetary systems was under pressure.

## What makes Bitcoin different

Bitcoin has:

- fixed supply schedule
- open-source code
- global network
- no central issuer
- transparent settlement rules

## The white paper

The Bitcoin white paper explains a system for sending value online without relying on a trusted third party.

Key ideas:

- peer-to-peer transactions
- proof of work
- timestamping
- chain of blocks
- limited trust in intermediaries

## Halving

Bitcoin halving is when the new supply issued to miners is reduced by half.

Why it matters:

- new supply decreases
- scarcity narrative increases
- market cycles often pay attention to it

Important: halving does not guarantee price increases.

## Basic on-chain metrics

Beginner-friendly metrics:

- active addresses
- transaction volume
- exchange balances
- long-term holder supply
- mining hash rate

Use metrics as context, not as guaranteed signals.

## Member action

Study Bitcoin in this order:

1. Why it was created.
2. How supply works.
3. What self-custody means.
4. What volatility looks like.
5. How much risk you can emotionally handle.
