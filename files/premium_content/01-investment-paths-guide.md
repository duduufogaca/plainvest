# Premium Guide: Investment Paths

## Purpose

This guide helps members understand the main investment paths shown in Plainvest and what each one is commonly used for.

## Index funds and ETFs

Index funds and ETFs are often the simplest starting point because they spread money across many companies in one purchase.

Useful for:

- long-term growth
- diversification
- simple recurring contributions
- avoiding single-company risk

Watch out for:

- market drops still happen
- fees vary between funds
- some ETFs are broad, while others are narrow or thematic

Questions to ask:

- What index does this fund track?
- What is the annual fee?
- Is it broad or concentrated?
- Is it available in my country?

## Stocks

Stocks are ownership pieces of individual companies.

Useful for:

- learning how businesses grow
- dividend income in some cases
- higher return potential

Watch out for:

- one company can perform badly
- research takes time
- emotions can become stronger than logic

Beginner rule: understand index funds first, then individual stocks.

## Bonds and fixed income

Bonds and fixed-income products are generally used for stability and income.

Useful for:

- lower volatility
- cash-flow planning
- balancing a portfolio

Watch out for:

- returns may be lower than shares
- inflation can reduce real return
- rules differ by country

## REITs and FIIs

REITs and FIIs allow investors to get property exposure without buying a whole property.

Useful for:

- income-focused investing
- property exposure
- diversification outside normal shares

Watch out for:

- interest rates affect property funds
- payouts can change
- property sectors have different risks

## Gold

Gold is usually treated as a hedge, not a growth engine.

Useful for:

- inflation fear
- currency weakness
- portfolio insurance

Watch out for:

- no dividends
- price can stay flat for long periods
- best used as part of a broader plan

## Bitcoin and crypto

Bitcoin and crypto are high-risk assets. They require extra education before investing.

Useful for:

- learning about scarce digital assets
- understanding alternative money systems
- high-risk/high-volatility allocation

Watch out for:

- 70%+ drawdowns have happened
- exchange and wallet mistakes can be permanent
- scams are common

## Member action

Pick one path. Learn it deeply. Use the simulator to compare it against other paths. Write down three questions before making any decision.
