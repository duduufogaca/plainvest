Premium Reading Path

Build conviction with better books.

The book list is not only a shopping list. It is a sequence: mindset first, diversified investing next, then Bitcoin, inflation, macro, business, and risk.

Recommended order

  1. The Psychology of Money for behaviour, patience, and decision-making.
  2. The Little Book of Common Sense Investing for index fund simplicity.
  3. A Random Walk Down Wall Street for market evidence and diversification.
  4. The Bitcoin Standard and Inventing Bitcoin for Bitcoin foundations.
  5. The Price of Tomorrow for technology, deflation, and money questions.
  6. The Black Swan for risk, uncertainty, and events people underestimate.

Study categories

Beginner investing

Index funds, ETFs, diversification, fees, compounding, and long-term behaviour.

Bitcoin and crypto

Money history, fixed supply, custody, cycles, security, and why scams look convincing.

Inflation and macro

Purchasing power, central banks, debt cycles, currency weakness, and uncertainty.

Business and wealth

How companies grow, why cash flow matters, and how ownership can build long-term wealth.
