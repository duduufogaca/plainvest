# Premium Guide: Crypto Cycle Lessons

## Why cycles matter

Crypto markets often move through emotional cycles:

- disbelief
- optimism
- euphoria
- fear
- capitulation
- boredom
- recovery

Understanding cycles helps members avoid emotional decisions.

## Bull market mistakes

Common mistakes:

- thinking gains are skill
- using too much leverage
- buying because everyone is talking about it
- ignoring taxes
- never taking notes or profits
- believing every narrative

## Bear market mistakes

Common mistakes:

- panic selling without a plan
- abandoning education
- assuming everything is dead
- forgetting why the asset exists
- holding weak assets only because they are down

## Better cycle rules

Use rules before emotions arrive:

- decide max allocation
- write why you bought
- define when you will review
- keep emergency money separate
- avoid leverage
- record every transaction

## Member action

Create a cycle plan:

- What would make me buy?
- What would make me stop buying?
- What would make me sell?
- What would make me wait?
- What mistake am I most likely to make?
