Prepare before the meeting

  1. Write your top three questions. Keep them specific.
  2. Write what country you live in and which platform you are considering.
  3. Write your current experience level: total beginner, used a platform before, or already investing.
  4. Bring screenshots only if needed, and hide personal details.
  5. Never share passwords, 2FA codes, seed phrases, private keys, or full identity documents.

Question templates

Investment path

“I am comparing X and Y. What are the main risks, fees, and beginner mistakes I should understand first?”

Platform setup

“I am considering this platform. What settings, fees, documents, and security features should I check before using it?”

Crypto safety

“I want to learn self-custody. What should I practice with a small amount before storing more value?”

Learning plan

“Based on my current level, which guide, book, or subject should I study next?”

After the call

  • Write the three clearest takeaways while they are fresh.
  • Choose one next action: read, compare, set up security, or keep learning.
  • Do not make a financial decision during emotional excitement right after the call.
  • Save new questions for support or a future extra call if needed.

Boundaries

The call is educational support. It is not personal financial advice, tax advice, legal advice, or a promise of investment results. The purpose is to help you understand options, risks, tools, and next questions more clearly.
