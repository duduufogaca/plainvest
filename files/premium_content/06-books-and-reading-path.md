# Premium Guide: Books and Reading Path

## Purpose

Books help members build judgment slowly. The goal is not to read everything at once. The goal is to read the right book at the right stage.

## Beginner order

1. The Psychology of Money
2. The Little Book of Common Sense Investing
3. I Will Teach You to Be Rich

Focus:

- behavior
- consistency
- index funds
- automation

## Market understanding

4. A Random Walk Down Wall Street
5. The Intelligent Investor

Focus:

- market efficiency
- diversification
- valuation
- risk discipline

## Bitcoin and money

6. Inventing Bitcoin
7. The Bitcoin Standard
8. The Fiat Standard
9. The Internet of Money

Focus:

- why Bitcoin exists
- monetary history
- inflation
- digital scarcity

## Macro, risk, business, and life

10. The Black Swan
11. Principles: Life and Work

Focus:

- rare events
- risk
- decision-making
- business thinking

## How to read

For every book, write:

- one idea I understood
- one idea I disagree with
- one action I might take
- one question for my Zoom call

## Member action

Choose only one book this month. Finish it before adding another.
