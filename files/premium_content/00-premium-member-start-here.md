# Plainvest Premium: Start Here

## What this membership is for

Plainvest Premium is built for people who understand that investing matters, but still feel unsure about the first practical steps.

The goal is not to tell members what to buy. The goal is to help members understand:

- what the main investment options are
- how each option works
- what risks to check before acting
- how to compare platforms and fees
- how to build a simple learning path
- what questions to bring to the included support call

## Recommended order

1. Read the DCA method guide.
2. Read the investment paths guide.
3. Choose one investment type to understand deeper.
4. Use the exchange setup checklist before opening or using a platform.
5. Read the Bitcoin and self-custody guides before touching crypto.
6. Use the book path when you want deeper confidence.
7. Use the chart reading guide only after you understand the basics.
8. Prepare questions for your 60-minute Zoom call.

## Important note

Plainvest is educational. It does not provide personal financial advice or tell members what to buy. Members should consider their goals, risk tolerance, fees, tax, time horizon, and personal circumstances before making decisions.
