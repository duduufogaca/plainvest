Australia Basics

Tax, CGT, and records without the panic.

This guide is educational only, not tax advice. Its job is to help Australian beginners understand the questions to ask before tax time.

The simple idea

In Australia, investing can create taxable events. The most common ones are dividends/distributions, interest, and capital gains when you sell. You do not need to become a tax expert before investing, but you do need clean records and a basic understanding of what the ATO may care about.

Capital Gains Tax in plain English

What triggers CGT?

Selling shares, ETFs, crypto, or units for more than you paid. Swapping crypto for another crypto can also count as disposal.

What is the gain?

Sale proceeds minus cost base. Cost base can include purchase price and some directly related costs.

12-month discount

Individuals may be eligible for a 50% CGT discount if they hold an asset for at least 12 months before disposal.

Losses

Capital losses can generally offset capital gains, but not ordinary income. Keep records even when something loses money.

Records to keep

  • Broker contract notes and trade confirmations.
  • Dividend and ETF distribution statements.
  • CSV exports from brokers and crypto exchanges.
  • Deposit and withdrawal history.
  • Dates, quantities, prices, fees, and exchange rates for non-AUD assets.
  • Notes about transfers between your own wallets or platforms.

Common beginner mistakes

  1. Thinking tax only matters when money leaves the platform.
  2. Forgetting that ETF distributions can include different tax components.
  3. Buying on several platforms without one master spreadsheet.
  4. Ignoring crypto-to-crypto swaps and wallet transfers.
  5. Waiting until June to reconstruct a year of activity.

What to ask an accountant

Ask how CGT applies to your situation, how to handle ETFs and foreign income, whether the 12-month discount applies, how to record crypto, and what software or reports they prefer. Plainvest can help you prepare better questions, but a registered tax professional should handle personal tax advice.
