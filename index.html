The hidden risk
Your savings are losing value right now.

Inflation silently erodes the purchasing power of money sitting in savings. $10,000 today won't buy the same things in 10 years — unless your money grows faster than inflation.

Purchasing power over a 30-year horizon

Compare how the same amount behaves in savings, invested growth, and real buying power after inflation.

What inflation means

Inflation is the rise in everyday prices over time, so the same cash buys less each year unless it grows faster than prices.

Plainvest uses adjustable average inflation assumptions for education. Official inflation is usually updated monthly or quarterly, not live every second, so you can change the percentage to test different scenarios.
